# Code of conduct

Tolkovo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its full text applies to every project space: the repository, issues, Discussions, chats and events.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing other people's private information are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub (see the repository's contact options). Reports are handled confidentially; maintainers who are involved in a report step aside from handling it.
